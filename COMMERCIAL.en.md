# Licensing and commercial use

[中文](COMMERCIAL.md) · **English**

The source code of XXD UI Kit is public. **Noncommercial use is free. Commercial use needs a paid license from XXD (小小东) first.**

The license is [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE). This page explains it in plain words; the `LICENSE` text is what counts.

## Free

- Learning, research and hobby projects.
- Personal projects that charge nothing and show no ads, such as a small extension for yourself or a personal home page.
- Use by schools, charities and government bodies for their own work.
- Opening issues and suggesting improvements to this project.

You can change and share it freely, as long as the `LICENSE` (or its URL) and the two `Required Notice` lines at the top go with it.

## Needs a commercial license first

When in doubt, anything that touches one of these is commercial:

- Used in a **paid** product or service: a paid extension, a membership site, a paid mini program, a SaaS.
- Used in an extension, site or mini program **with ads or affiliate sales**.
- Used by a **company or team** for its work, including internal-only tools and admin pages.
- **Client work**: contracting, freelancing, agency projects.
- **Selling** it or something made from it: templates, themes, course material.
- **Training or fine-tuning a commercial model** on it, or building a commercial AI generation service with it.

## How to get a license

Go to **<https://ui.xiaoxiaodong.ai/en/license>**. Scan the QR code to add XXD on WeChat, or send a DM on X. Tell us three things:

1. What the product is.
2. Whether it's a company or a person.
3. Roughly how many users it has.

Price and scope (one product, a whole company, a client project) depend on the case. Once agreed, we sign a written license.

## Common questions

**What about versions I got under MIT?** Versions up to and including 2026-11-01 (commit `8aa80f12` and earlier) were released under MIT. If you already have one of those versions, you can keep using it under MIT. Every later version is under the current license.

**What about the code from Kumo?** The original code from Cloudflare's [Kumo](https://github.com/cloudflare/kumo) in the React components stays under MIT (see [LICENSE-KUMO](LICENSE-KUMO)). XXD's restyling and additions are under the current license.

**I'm just playing with it now and may charge later. Is that OK?** Yes, go ahead. Get a license before you start charging, add ads, or bring it into a company.

**What happens to code I contribute?** See [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.en.md): by contributing, you agree that XXD may include it in commercial licenses.
